# Lockeroom Technical Initiatives - Executive Summary

### 🚀 Lockeroom Technical Initiatives

**1. Session Attendance Tracking (Heat Map)**
• **What it is:** A real-time dashboard that instantly surfaces client attendance data so managers can spot drop-offs.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 90 minutes per week (per manager).
• **The Impact:** Protects ~$80k/year in at-risk revenue by enabling early intervention, while completely eliminating manual data digging.

**2. InBody Weight & Body Fat % Graphs**
• **What it is:** Automated InBody metrics attached directly into the 360 dashboard.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 67.5 minutes per week (per coach).
• **The Impact:** Recoups over an hour of coaching capacity per week by eliminating the need to hunt through PDFs and workbooks.

**3. New Sale Form (Client Journey Failsafe)**
• **What it is:** An n8n automation flow that handles database updates, tracking sheets, and team notifications for every new sale.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** ~75 minutes per week (sales/admin team).
• **The Impact:** Cuts new client processing time from 20 minutes down to 5 minutes, ensuring zero dropped balls during onboarding.

**4. Schedule Preferences (12-Week Rolling Portal)**
• **What it is:** A transparent Retool heat map where coaches log preferences, replacing manual spreadsheet management.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 60 minutes per week (Jarryd & Andy).
• **The Impact:** Eliminates the friction, resentment, and manual back-and-forth of the weekly schedule Tetris.

**5. Coach Weekly Snapshots (Expectations vs. Actuals)**
• **What it is:** An automated dashboard tracking expected rostered hours versus actually delivered coaching hours.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** ~30 minutes per week (management review).
• **The Impact:** Creates total transparency and fairness, resolving performance ambiguity and roster disputes instantly.

**6. Boxing Metrics Visibility Dashboard**
• **What it is:** A daily live dashboard tracking boxing profitability, new clients, and trial conversions.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 60 minutes per week (Management, Liam, Yam).
• **The Impact:** Replaces delayed, inaccurate monthly reflections with real-time data for proactive decision-making.

**7. Nutrition Consultation Automation (Gemini & Gem)**
• **What it is:** An automated workflow using Gemini transcripts and Gem to instantly generate and send post-consultation client emails.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** ~75 minutes per week (coaching team).
• **The Impact:** Completely removes the tedious manual copy-pasting of Fireflies summaries, freeing coaches up faster after every consult.

**8. Cleaning Margin & Hourly Tracker (Joseph)**
• **What it is:** A dynamic sheet that instantly calculates cleaning vendor costs, task trade-offs, and facility margins.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 90 minutes per week (Joseph).
• **The Impact:** Empowers leadership to make instant, data-driven decisions on vendor contracts without paying for manual accounting math.

**9. BioMap Supplementation Database (Tadhg & Yam)**
• **What it is:** A Retool database built to store and instantly retrieve Canva descriptions and supplement links.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** ~30 minutes per week (during reporting cycles).
• **The Impact:** Replaces manual typing with a seamless copy-paste system, laying the exact groundwork for 100% automated PDF generation in the future.

**10. Staff Onboarding (Google NotebookLM)**
• **What it is:** An interactive AI system that trains new hires on the nutrition notebook independently.
• **Time Saved:** 2 hours per new hire (No weekly time attached).
• **The Impact:** Slashes senior coach teaching time from 2.5 hours down to 30 minutes per onboarding, protecting highly-paid management capacity.

**11. Call Accountability Dashboard & Renewal Tracker Upgrade**
• **What it is:** Upgraded dashboards with personalized, one-click tabulated views to efficiently track daily sales calls and membership renewals.
• **Key Upgrades:** 
  - *Call Accountability:* Hardcoded default filters for Aaron, Sam, and the revenue team (eliminating 3–4 manual condition/timeframe adjustments per session).
  - *Renewal Tracker:* Default timeframe (1 month past, 3 months future) and auto-assignment filters built directly into individual tabs for instant access.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 40 minutes per week (manager time).
• **The Impact:** Eliminates repetitive, multi-step filter setups. While minor per session, the high frequency of daily use compounds, directly recovering nearly an hour of management review time every single week.

**12. Referral Tracking Data Extraction**
• **What it is:** Using Cursor to instantly query and segment referral data for targeted email and WhatsApp outreach.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** ~30 minutes per week (2 hours per month for Andy/Connor).
• **The Impact:** Turns a 2-hour manual data mining task into a 5-minute query, completely protecting coaching schedule capacity from being blocked off for admin work.

**13. Coach Workbook Adjustments v2 (Attendance Integration)**
• **What it is:** Removing deprecated individual metrics (replaced by the automated InBody graphs) and injecting critical session attendance data directly into the personal training view.
• **Weekly Time Saved:** 30 minutes per week (1.5 minutes × 20 coaches).
• **The Impact:** Delivers a twofold effect: it saves half an hour of weekly admin cross-referencing while forcing a sharper, front-of-mind focus on the crucial member health ratings related to session attendance and usage.
